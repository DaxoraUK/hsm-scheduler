# Ground Control visible UX pass 1

## Purpose

This pass removes repeated or misleading information from the two most frequently used workspaces without changing scheduling behaviour.

## Mission Control

- Removed the four-card promotional insight grid.
- Removed generic grant and future bolt-on marketing from the operational dashboard.
- Kept actionable status, guided workflow, ground state, weather, activity and timeline information.
- Renamed `Platform Readiness` to `Matchday Readiness` because the percentage measures the selected operational workflow, not SaaS launch readiness.
- Simplified the command-strip wording to describe its actual purpose.

## Operations

- Removed the large static `Weekend Operations` wrapper banner.
- Removed the unconditional `Ground Ready` label, which could conflict with live closures, unresolved fixtures or missing officials.
- Kept the operational tabs and each workspace's own contextual header.
- Reworded Saturday and Sunday tab descriptions so neither is treated as primary or secondary.
- Clarified the Operations Centre, Midweek and Timeline responsibilities.

## Information architecture rule established

- Mission Control shows current position and next action.
- Operations contains controls and detailed resolution work.
- Analytics explains trends and evidence.
- Reports produces formal, exportable outputs.
- Marketing and future-product messages do not belong inside live operational dashboards.
