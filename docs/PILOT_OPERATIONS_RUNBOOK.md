# Ground Control Pilot Operations Runbook

## Pilot entry criteria

A club can enter validation when:

- an accountable owner is named;
- teams, pitches, venues and parking are verified;
- the operator has completed onboarding;
- required legal acceptance is complete;
- support and escalation contacts are confirmed;
- the chosen package or pilot exemption is recorded.

## Pilot stages

- **Candidate:** discovery only; no delivery commitment.
- **Invited:** club has agreed to prepare for the pilot.
- **Onboarding:** workspace configuration and training underway.
- **Validation:** real workflows are being tested before operational reliance.
- **Live pilot:** club uses Ground Control for agreed operational scope.
- **Paused:** pilot temporarily stopped with a documented reason.
- **Graduated:** exit criteria achieved and commercial next step agreed.
- **Withdrawn:** pilot closed without graduation.

## Operating cadence

During a live pilot:

- agree one named club contact and one Daxora owner;
- hold a short weekly review;
- log every defect or support request as a support case;
- capture severity, workaround and release target;
- avoid adding requested bolt-ons during launch hardening;
- record measurable time saved, errors prevented and decisions improved;
- review pilot health after every serious incident.

## Exit criteria

A pilot should graduate only when:

- core workflows are used successfully across multiple matchweeks;
- no unresolved P0 or P1 defect remains;
- support procedures work;
- data can be exported and reported;
- the club confirms operational value;
- the commercial plan and billing route are agreed.
