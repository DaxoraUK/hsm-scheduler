# Contact and negotiation playbook

## Objective

Secure a confidential strategic-acquisition discussion, not an ordinary sales demonstration or supplier listing.

## Target participants

- TeamFeePay CEO/co-founder.
- TeamFeePay CTO or senior technical decision-maker.
- Product leader responsible for club operating-system expansion.
- Corporate-development/investor representative after management interest is established.

Verify current role-holders immediately before outreach.

## Positioning

Use:

> Daxora can accelerate TeamFeePay's move from club administration into physical operations, facility planning and league infrastructure.

Avoid:

- accusations that TeamFeePay is copying Daxora;
- discussion of retirement or tax motivation;
- unsupported claims of exclusivity or patent protection;
- claiming a live TeamFeePay integration;
- raw code volume as the main value argument;
- naming the minimum acceptable price.

## First-call goals

1. Confirm the strategic roadmap overlap.
2. Identify the executive sponsor.
3. Understand whether acquisition, exclusive licence or partnership is viable.
4. Secure a technical/product follow-up.
5. Agree mutual NDA and diligence process.

Do not negotiate detailed price before the buyer has acknowledged strategic fit and requested diligence.

## Negotiation anchor

The founder's private opening position should be prepared separately and reviewed with advisers. Consideration should distinguish:

- cash at completion;
- fixed deferred consideration;
- measurable earn-out;
- narrowly defined revenue participation;
- paid transition support;
- any separate employment or consultancy arrangement.

Never use the term “kickback.” Use royalty, revenue participation, contingent consideration or earn-out.

## Deal protections to seek

- majority of value paid at completion;
- objective earn-out milestones;
- no buyer discretion to prevent milestones;
- audit rights for revenue participation;
- bundle-allocation rules;
- change-of-control acceleration;
- treatment if the product is shelved;
- clear IP acceptance process;
- limited transition term and hours;
- employment separated from purchase price;
- tax treatment reviewed before heads of terms become binding.
