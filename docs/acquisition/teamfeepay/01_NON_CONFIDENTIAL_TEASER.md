# Daxora Ground Control
## Strategic product acquisition opportunity for TeamFeePay

**Confidentiality level:** suitable for initial non-confidential discussion.
**Do not attach source code, database details or implementation algorithms.**

## The opportunity

TeamFeePay has established the member, payment, registration, safeguarding, communication and club-administration relationship. Daxora Ground Control adds a complementary operating layer for the physical and competition work that football clubs and leagues still coordinate through spreadsheets, messages and disconnected calendars.

Daxora is a working multi-organisation product, not a presentation concept. It has been designed from real grassroots football operations and includes club, facility, coach and league workflows in one model.

## What Daxora adds

- Fixture ingestion, matchday planning and pitch allocation.
- Pitch suitability, split areas, capacity rules and time buffers.
- Closures, weather disruption, postponements and recovery options.
- Annual training, friendly, winter-site and one-off booking planning.
- Coach requests, approvals, shared calendars and operational alerts.
- Officials, referee pools and assistant requirements.
- League divisions, playing calendars, fixture generation and cups.
- Registrations, eligibility, discipline, league finance and club portals.
- Unified analytics covering matches, training, friendlies, external sites, downtime and unused capacity.
- Grant and facilities evidence derived from actual operational data.

## Strategic logic

The acquisition would allow TeamFeePay to accelerate from club administration into deeper operational control without repeating the full cycle of grassroots discovery, facility modelling, workflow design, database evolution and edge-case testing already completed in Daxora.

Daxora can be:

1. operated as a separately deployed integrated service;
2. connected to TeamFeePay through a controlled partner API;
3. progressively absorbed into TeamFeePay's preferred technical stack; or
4. used as a validated specification and domain-logic asset for a managed port.

## Demonstration

A private synthetic demonstration shows:

1. authorised club, team and coach records entering Daxora;
2. TeamFeePay events receiving facility and operating intelligence;
3. one pitch closure identifying all affected fixtures and audiences;
4. recommended recovery options preventing cancellation;
5. updated event statuses and facility analytics returning to the partner platform.

No live TeamFeePay or Horwich St Mary's personal data is used.

## Transaction intention

The founder is exploring a strategic acquisition of the software, intellectual property, product research, documentation and transition knowledge. This is not a funding request and no production TeamFeePay access is assumed.
