# TeamFeePay strategic fit

## Current public position

As of 21 July 2026, TeamFeePay publicly positions itself as a football-specific club-management platform spanning payments, registrations, scheduling, communications, safeguarding, governance, fundraising and club development. YFM Equity Partners describes its £4.5 million investment as supporting continued product expansion, UK market leadership and European growth. TeamFeePay publicly states that it partners with 27 County Football Associations.

These facts make Daxora relevant because the product extends the same operating-system direction into facility, matchday, annual-planning and league infrastructure.

## Why acquisition can be faster than internal build

An internal build would still require TeamFeePay to discover and validate:

- pitch hierarchy and split-resource modelling;
- cross-event capacity and time-buffer rules;
- club-versus-league data authority;
- closure and recovery workflows;
- coach preference and approval models;
- winter and external-site planning;
- full-pitch versus area consumption;
- official pools and assistant requirements;
- fixture-generation constraints and competition rules;
- operational analytics definitions;
- grant-evidence interpretation;
- multi-tenant security and audit expectations.

Daxora packages those decisions with working interfaces, migrations, tests and documented product history.

## Important boundary

No public TeamFeePay developer specification has been located. The acquisition demo therefore uses a synthetic provider adapter and a stable Daxora partner contract. A production connection must be designed jointly after TeamFeePay supplies written authorisation, permitted data scopes, endpoint details, authentication, rate limits and webhook rules.
