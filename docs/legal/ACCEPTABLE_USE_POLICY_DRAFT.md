# Daxora Ground Control Acceptable Use Policy — Draft

**Status:** Draft for professional legal review.

The policy should prohibit:

- Unauthorised access, credential sharing or attempts to bypass club isolation and permissions.
- Uploading unlawful, abusive, discriminatory, malicious or infringing material.
- Using Ground Control to send spam or unlawful direct marketing.
- Interfering with service availability, probing security controls or introducing malware.
- Scraping, reverse engineering or excessive automated access outside agreed integrations.
- Uploading unnecessary sensitive or special-category personal data.
- Misrepresenting fixture, safeguarding or official information.

It should explain proportionate investigation, suspension, evidence preservation and appeal/contact routes.
