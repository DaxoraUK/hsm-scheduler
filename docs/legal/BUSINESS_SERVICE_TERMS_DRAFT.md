# Daxora Ground Control Business Service Terms — Draft

**Status:** Draft for professional legal review. Do not publish or use for checkout until reviewed and completed.

This document should cover, at minimum:

- The legal contracting party: the sole trader’s full name trading as Daxora, together with an address where legal documents can be served.
- The customer: the club or organisation purchasing Ground Control and the authority of the accepting account owner.
- The subscribed service, selected package, permitted users and applicable usage limits.
- Monthly or annual charges, payment dates, failed-payment handling, taxes, price changes and invoicing.
- Trial terms, renewal, cancellation, suspension, read-only access and data export/deletion arrangements.
- Customer responsibilities for fixture, team, volunteer and venue data.
- Service availability, maintenance, support scope and exclusions.
- Intellectual property, confidentiality, acceptable use and third-party services.
- Liability allocation, indemnities where appropriate, force majeure and governing law.
- How changes to the terms are notified and how historic accepted versions are retained.

Ground Control’s application records the exact published version accepted by the club owner. This draft does not replace advice from a UK solicitor.
