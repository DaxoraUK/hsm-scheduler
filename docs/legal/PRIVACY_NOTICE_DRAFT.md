# Daxora Privacy Notice — Draft

**Status:** Draft for professional privacy review. This is a transparency notice, not a consent form.

The notice should identify the sole trader, contact details and the purposes/lawful bases for processing, including:

- Account creation, authentication and workspace security.
- Subscription administration, invoices and payment records.
- Product operation, audit logging, support and service improvement.
- Communications requested by customers and essential service notices.
- Legal, tax, fraud-prevention and security obligations.

It should list data categories, recipients/subprocessors, international transfers, retention periods, individual rights, complaint routes and the ICO. Separate the data for which Daxora is controller from data processed on club instructions.
